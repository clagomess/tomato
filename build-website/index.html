Why Tomato?

A focused tool that keeps your work yours.

Local and Offline

Your data stays on your disk and the app works without an internet connection. No sign-up, no login, no cloud workspace. Just open it and start working.

Git-Friendly

Collections and environments are readable JSON, one file per item. Version them in git, review real diffs, or share the folder with your team through any repository or shared drive.

Encrypted Secrets

Tokens and passwords are kept in encrypted KeePass vaults, separate from the rest of your config. You can commit your environments without leaking credentials.

Import and Export

Bring your Postman collections in and export them back out at any time. Switching tools is never a migration project.

Code Snippets

Turn any request into ready-to-paste code, such as a curl command, in one click.

Light and Fast

A native desktop app with no embedded browser. Large responses stream to disk instead of memory, so big payloads won't freeze the app.
